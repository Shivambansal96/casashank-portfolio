Chartered Accountant Services

Looking for a CA in Siliguri? Sashank Agarwal & Co assists individuals, professionals, startups, and businesses with income tax returns, GST registration and filing, audit support, company formation, accounting, payroll, and financial planning.

Income Tax Planning

Income tax planning and ITR filing for individuals, professionals, and businesses, including TDS returns, assessment support, and tax notice assistance.

GST & Tax Compliance

GST registration and return filing, including GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, with input tax credit, refund, and GST notice support.

Audit & Assurance

Audit and assurance services including statutory, tax, internal, bank, and stock audits, with financial statement and compliance review.

Business Consultation

Business planning for startups and established enterprises, with support for market entry, operational efficiency, cost control, and growth strategy.

Financial Advisory

Financial planning for individuals and businesses, including cash-flow management, budgeting, forecasting, and financial risk review.

Retainership Services

Ongoing accounting, tax, payroll, and compliance support with periodic reviews, financial reporting, and year-round advisory.

Company Registration

Business formation guidance in India for private limited companies, one-person companies, LLPs, partnership firms, and proprietorships.

Bookkeeping & Accounting

Bookkeeping and accounting support including transaction recording, bank reconciliation, payables, receivables, and financial statements.

Payroll Management

Salary processing and payroll compliance support covering PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS deductions, payroll reports, and Form 16.

Corporate Compliance

ROC annual filing and corporate compliance support, including statutory registers, board documentation, and company-law records.

Litigation Support

Tax dispute and appeal support for income tax and GST matters, including notices and assistance with proceedings before relevant authorities.

Business Taxation

Direct and indirect tax planning for businesses, including business tax returns, TDS compliance, tax management, and tax health checks.

Trust & NGO Services

Registration and compliance support for trusts, societies, and NGOs, including 12A and 80G applications, annual filings, accounts, and audit support.
